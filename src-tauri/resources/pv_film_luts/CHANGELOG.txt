Pairview film looks, baked from spektrafilm 0.3.4 by Andrea Volpato
https://github.com/andreavolpato/spektrafilm

Made with scripts/pv-bake-film-looks.py: V-Log in, sRGB out, one LUT, 25 points. Not modified
after baking. Licence: SPEKTRAFILM_LICENSE.txt in this folder.

Difference from the reference bake: film exposure compensation, so each look lands near the
brightness of a Sony A7C II's own files from a RAW with its usual highlight headroom. At 0 EV these
would match spektrafilm's standard LUTs (checked: K-Portra 400 is identical to RapidRAW's).
Slide films have no print stage; the header's "Print" line is unused for them.
